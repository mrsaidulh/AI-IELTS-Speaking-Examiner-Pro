PART 1

Examiner: Where are you from?

Candidate: I'm from Bangladesh, but currently I live in Mymensingh. I really like the city because it is relatively peaceful and the environment is quite fresh.

Examiner: What do you like most about your hometown?

Candidate: The thing I like most is the environment. There are many green areas and the city is not extremely crowded. Also, people are generally friendly.


PART 2

Examiner: Describe a place you enjoy visiting.

Candidate: There is a riverside area that I really enjoy visiting. I normally go there at the weekend, particularly in the evening. I like this place because the environment is calm and I can get away from the busy lifestyle. Sometimes I sit in the park and enjoy the gentle breeze from the river. It makes me feel refreshed.


PART 3

Examiner: Why do people enjoy visiting different places?

Candidate: I think people visit different places mainly because they want to experience something new. For example, if someone lives in a busy city, they might want to go to a quiet natural place. It can also help people reduce stress and spend quality time with their family.

Examiner: Do you think tourism is always beneficial?

Candidate: Not necessarily. Tourism can bring money and create jobs, but if too many tourists visit one place, it can damage the environment. Therefore, I think governments need to manage tourism properly.
